description=Keep the lights on after the grid and the gas are gone, with what a Kentucky farm, cabin or RV owner could have had in 1993: solar panels, wind chargers, a treadmill, a bank of batteries, and a generator that runs on manure gas or wood. You start with a car battery in a wooden box, enough for the TV and a lamp. Every panel after that is one you found, fixed or built, every battery wears out, and the weather decides what you get.

description=[h2]Questions[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Can I add it to my save?[/b] Yes. Loot turns up where you haven't been yet; solar sites and garden lights anywhere.
description=[*][b]Can I remove it?[/b] Not cleanly: what it built into your world goes with it.
description=[*][b]Can I make it easier, or harder?[/b] Yes, on the [b]Renewable Energy[/b] sandbox page; each option says what it changes. Start with [b]Sunlight Multiplier[/b], [b]Battery Wear[/b] and [b]Storm Damage[/b].
description=[*][b]Why isn't my panel making power?[/b] Open [b]Status[/b] on its bank: a source short for a reason you can fix gets a red row saying why.
description=[*][b]Does it work before the power goes out?[/b] The bank stands by while the grid is on, filling its batteries, and takes over within minutes of the shutoff.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Everything you can find or build[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/items.png[/img]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Banks[/b] hold the batteries and what you wire into them: inverters, charge controllers, a charger and a [b]Solar Failsafe[/b] that starts the generator when they run low.
description=[*][b]Batteries wear[/b] with every deep drain. Service them, recondition them less far each time, then strip and rebuild them with plates you cast yourself.
description=[*][b]Panels follow the sun[/b]: flat ones for summer, wall panels for winter, ground racks for the year. Storms crack modules; seal them with epoxy or glue, or reglaze them.
description=[*][b]Wind chargers, treadmills, digesters and gasifiers[/b] fill in when the sun won't.
description=[*][b]Small gadgets[/b] need no bank: a maintainer for a parked car's battery, a charger for flashlight batteries, and garden lights.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Getting started[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/bank.png[/img]
description=
description=[olist]
description=[*]Craft a battery box and [b]Place Object[/b] indoors.
description=[*]Right-click it and [b]Wire In[/b] batteries and an inverter.
description=[*][b]Connect Bank[/b], like a generator: Electrical 3, or the generator magazine.
description=[*]Put up a panel outside, then [b]Connect Sources in Reach[/b].
description=[*][b]Turn On[/b]. [b]Status[/b] shows what it's doing, and which sources need fixing.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=The bank powers everything a generator in its spot would, the neighbours' fridges included, so set up away from town.
description=
description=[h2]Wind[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/wind.png[/img]
description=
description=Build them from [b]Welding[/b], or three in wood from [b]Carpentry[/b]. A scoop rotor turns in any breeze and can't break. A treadmill motor, car alternator or homemade one makes real power, most in winter, via a charge controller. Set them high, clear of trees and buildings, 4 squares apart, or they rob each other's wind.
description=
description=When a gale sets the blades creaking, [b]Set the Brake[/b] or replace them: wood, plastic pipe, or forged steel that breaks last.
description=
description=[h2]Treadmills[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/treadmill.png[/img]
description=
description=No sun, no wind? [b]Connect to a Battery Bank[/b] from the treadmill's menu, then [b]Run on Treadmill[/b]: half an hour banks about what a fridge-freezer draws in that time, at the cost of endurance. Taken apart, it gives up the motor wind chargers are built around.
description=
description=[h2]Biogas[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/biogas.png[/img]
description=
description=Feed a digester manure and scraps, keep it above 10 °C, and it makes gas. Overfeed it and it sours until you add wood ash or baking soda; indoors, it can poison the house, and it stinks. [b]Install Gas Conversion Kit[/b] on a generator, [b]Connect Gas Line[/b] with a rubber hose for every five squares, and [b]Run on Gas[/b]: its tank stays as it was. A tire pump or a fridge compressor bottles it.
description=
description=[h2]Wood gas[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/woodgas.png[/img]
description=
description=FEMA's 1989 plans run an engine on wood: a gasifier from a trash can, a steel drum or welded sheet metal, right beside a kitted generator. Fill it with wood or charcoal and light it, clean out the ash, change the filter packing before the tar gets through, and keep it outdoors.
description=
description=[h2]Crafting[/h2]
description=Learn recipes from the Homestead Power magazines and FEMA's plans, by levelling their skills, or by researching a part you found. Most need a table and light; battery rebuilds need a furnace, a press and a kiln. A makeshift inverter does until you find a real one. Every recipe is on [url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/recipes.png]one sheet[/url].
description=
description=[h2]Where to look[/h2]
description=Think about who had this in 1993: campers and boaters, farmers, electricians, the phone and power companies, road crews and preppers, and who sold it: electronics and camping stores. Cracked modules are common, good ones aren't. Some places come already set up, panels usually on the roof, so bring a way up. Barns and hunting shacks sometimes hide worn gear out back.
