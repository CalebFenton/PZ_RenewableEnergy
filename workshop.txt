description=Keep the lights on after the grid and the gas are gone, with the solar gear a Kentucky farm, cabin or RV owner could have had in 1993. You start with a car battery in a wooden box and an inverter that plugs into a lighter socket, enough for the TV and a lamp. Every panel after that is one you found, fixed or built, every battery wears out, and the weather decides what you get. Keep at it, and a few months in there's a room full of batteries, a yard of panels, and a generator you only start when it's been cloudy all week.
description=
description=[b]Build 42.20 or newer.[/b] Single player and multiplayer. Fine to add to a save in progress.
description=
description=[h2]How it plays[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/setup.png[/img]

description=[table][tr][th]When[/th][th]What you're after[/th][th]What it takes[/th][/tr][tr][td]The first weeks[/td][td]The TV, a lamp, a mini fridge[/td][td]A battery box, two car batteries, a plug-in inverter, two panels[/td][/tr][tr][td]A month or so in[/td][td]The kitchen fridge[/td][td]A battery rack of deep cycle batteries, a bigger inverter, a charge controller, a handful of panels[/td][/tr][tr][td]Months later[/td][td]The freezer, the washer, the microwave[/td][td]A bank of L-16s, an inverter/charger, two charge controllers, a failsafe on the generator, a yard of panels[/td][/tr][tr][td]The long haul[/td][td]The electric range and the dryer[/td][td]A metal bank, two inverter/chargers, four charge controllers, and every panel you can find[/td][/tr][/table]

description=Panels are what you'll spend the game chasing: you find a few, fix the cracked ones, and in the end build your own out of anything with solar cells in it.

description=[h2]Everything you can find or build[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/items.png[/img]
description=
description=It's the hardware they sold in 1993: small modules, marine and L-16 batteries, inverters for RVs and cabins.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Batteries wear[/b] with every deep drain: a deep cycle kept above half lasts years, a car battery run down every day is done in months. Service it with water and a rag or baking soda, or rebuild it: strip the plates, recast them at a furnace in a mold you fired yourself, and put it back together.
description=[*][b]Panels follow the sun[/b] as it moves over Knox County, checked against sunlight measured at Louisville. Flat panels make the most in summer and lose most of it by December, wall panels do best in winter, and ground racks over the year. Snow piles up on flat panels until you sweep it off, and thunderstorms crack modules now and then.
description=[*][b]The generator stays the backup.[/b] Plug it in beside the bank and, with a charger wired in, it fills the batteries while it runs; a [b]Solar Failsafe[/b] starts it when they run low and stops it when they're full.
description=[*][b]Small gadgets[/b] work without a bank: a maintainer that trickle-charges a parked car's battery, a charger for flashlight batteries, and garden lights. Some houses still have a row of them along the front walk:
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Getting started[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/bank.png[/img]
description=
description=[olist]
description=[*][b]Build a bank.[/b] Craft a battery box, right-click it and [b]Place Object[/b] indoors.
description=[*][b]Wire in the parts.[/b] Right-click the bank and [b]Wire In[/b] batteries and an inverter. It tells you what you're missing.
description=[*][b]Mount the panels.[/b] Craft a module into a flat or wall panel, or two onto a ground rack, and place it outside.
description=[*][b]Connect them.[/b] Right-click the bank and [b]Connect Panels in Reach[/b].
description=[*][b]Turn it on.[/b] Right-click the bank and [b]Turn On[/b]. [b]Status[/b] shows what it's doing, down to why each panel isn't at full sun.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=If what's switched on draws more than the inverters carry, the power cuts out until you switch something off. The bank powers everything a generator in its spot would, the neighbours' fridges and lights included, so switch those off or set up away from town.
description=
description=[h2]Crafting[/h2]
description=Most recipes need a table and enough light, and a battery rebuild a furnace, a hand press and a kiln too. You learn them from two Homestead Power magazines, by levelling Electrical, or by researching a part you found. The inverters, the maintainer and the battery charger can't be crafted: you'll have to find them.
description=
description=Every recipe's inputs, skills and tools are in the gallery, and on [url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/recipes.png]one sheet[/url].
description=
description=[h2]Where to look[/h2]
description=Think about who had this stuff in 1993: people with campers and boats, farmers, electricians, the phone and power companies, road crews and preppers. Cracked modules are common and good ones aren't; the biggest batteries and inverters are rare. Some places come already set up, from a stock tank pump in a pasture to a power company yard, though not every world has every one.
description=
description=The easiest start is on the road. Road crews and army checkpoints left solar arrow boards behind, still flashing:
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_RenewableEnergy/master/art/promo/images/trailer.png[/img]
description=
description=The box on the tongue holds deep cycle batteries and usually a charge controller, often behind a padlock, and the panels come off with a wrench and a little Mechanics. Strip it where it stands, or tow it home.
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Sunlight Multiplier[/b]: how much power panels make. Out of the box they make more than they really did; 1 is true 1993 output, when it took a yard of panels to run one fridge.
description=[*][b]Loot Rarity Multiplier[/b], one each for modules, batteries and everything else: how often the mod's items turn up.
description=[*][b]Battery Wear[/b]: how hard deep drains wear batteries. 0 turns wear off.
description=[*][b]Stash Houses[/b]: adds houses stocked with solar gear, and maps that lead to them. Set it before you start.
description=[/list]
description=
description=The rest, from storm damage to garden lights and solar sites, are on the [b]Renewable Energy[/b] page of the sandbox options.
description=
description=[h2]Questions[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Can I add it to my save?[/b] Yes. Loot and stocked rooms turn up where you haven't been yet; solar sites and garden lights appear anywhere.
description=[*][b]Can I remove it from a save?[/b] Not cleanly: what it built into your world goes with it.
description=[/list]
