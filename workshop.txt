title=Renewable Energy
description=Sooner or later the grid goes down. After that you either haul gas for a generator every few days, or you go out and find some solar gear.
description=
description=This mod gives you the solar gear a Kentucky household, farm or RV owner could actually have owned in 1993. You start with a car battery in a wooden box, one salvaged panel and a cheap inverter that plugs into a lighter socket. That runs a lamp and the radio. Later you'll have a rack of deep cycle batteries, a yard of tilted panels and a backup generator that starts itself when the batteries run low.
description=
description=[b]Build 42.20 or newer.[/b]
description=
description=[h2]How real is it?[/h2]
description=The sun moves the way it does over Knox County, worked out from the in-game date and time, and checked against sunlight measured at Louisville. By December a flat panel makes less than a third of what it did in July. Clouds, fog and rain cut it, snow piles up on flat panels until you sweep it off, and hot cells make a little less.
description=
description=Batteries wear faster the harder you drain them, hold less in the cold, and can crack if you leave a flat one out in a hard freeze. A fridge's start-up surge trips a small inverter. Wall panels only go on south and east walls, which is where you'd want them anyway.
description=
description=Some things bend for fun: panels make three times their real output by default (there's a sandbox slider), and the solar charger recharges flashlight batteries.
description=
description=[h2]Stages[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Scavenger:[/b] a Battery Box, a car battery, a 140 W Plug-in Inverter and a panel or two. Lights, the radio, the TV. Not a fridge.
description=[*][b]RV or cabin:[/b] a Battery Bank of deep cycle batteries with a 600 W Power Inverter and charge controllers. Runs a fridge-freezer and the lights.
description=[*][b]Homestead:[/b] a 2,500 W Inverter/Charger, L-16 batteries and a backup generator with a Solar Failsafe. Most of a house.
description=[/list]
description=A Solar Battery Maintainer keeps your car's battery topped up, and a running car can charge the bank through 4 lengths of wire. The deep cycle battery fits in a car, too, and runs the radio twice as long.
description=
description=[h2]Panels[/h2]
description=Ground racks hold two modules and make the most over a year. Flat panels are cheap and great in summer, bad in winter. Wall panels make about two thirds as much over a year but beat flat panels all winter.
description=
description=[h2]Finding it[/h2]
description=Look where people had this stuff in 1993: campers, auto parts stores, farms, electricians' vans, the phone and power company vans, and preppers' caches. The Homestead Power magazines teach the recipes, and you'll find those in electronics stores, bookstores and sometimes people's living rooms. Some sheds and garages come with a battery box someone already built, and there are four stash houses you can turn on in the sandbox options.
description=
