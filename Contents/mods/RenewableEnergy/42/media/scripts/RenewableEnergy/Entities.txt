/*
    What the build menu builds: the biogas digesters, the wood gas gasifiers and the wind chargers.
    A digester or gasifier tile becomes one through OnCreate
    (server/RenewableEnergy/Biogas/DigesterSystem.lua, server/RenewableEnergy/WoodGas/GasifierSystem.lua);
    a wind charger is a plain tile a bank connects to like a panel. In module Base with the mod's
    prefix, as the craftRecipes are (DEVELOPMENT.md, Scripts). A DisplayName is looked up, without
    its spaces, in Recipes.json.

    The digesters are taught by Homestead Power #3, the gasifiers by the FEMA pamphlet, or all are
    learned with the skills below.
*/
