/*
    What the build menu builds: the biogas digesters, the wood gas gasifiers and the wind chargers.
    A digester or gasifier tile becomes one through OnCreate (its server system); a wind charger
    is a plain tile a bank connects to like a panel. A bladed wind charger's one recipe takes any
    blades and builds on its wooden-blade tile; its OnCreate (Wind.lua) makes it the entity of the
    blades it took. In module Base with the mod's prefix, as the craftRecipes are. A DisplayName is
    looked up, without its spaces, in Recipes.json.

    The digesters are taught by Homestead Power #3, the gasifiers by the FEMA pamphlet, the wind
    chargers by Homestead Power #4, or all are learned with the skills below.
*/
