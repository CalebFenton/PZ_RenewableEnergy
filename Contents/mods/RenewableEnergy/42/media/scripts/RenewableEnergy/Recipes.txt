/*
    Craft recipes. Display names come from Translate/<LANG>/Recipes.json, keyed by recipe name,
    and a recipe's Tooltip from Tooltip.json. Callbacks are in
    lua/shared/RenewableEnergy/RecipeCode.lua.

    The recipes live in module Base, prefixed against name clashes: the game looks a recipe up
    by its bare name when researching an item and in a magazine's recipe list, and a bare name
    resolves only in Base (ScriptBucketCollection.getScript).

    Conventions follow vanilla's electrical recipes: tools mode:keep with
    flags[Prop1;MayDegradeLight] and a table within reach (AnySurfaceCraft). Base.BlowTorch
    inputs count uses on purpose. A battery input says flags[ItemCount], or it would count the
    battery's charge as uses.

    Fluids: an `item 1 [*]` line followed by `-fluid` takes that fluid from any container, and
    one followed by `+fluid` pours it into an empty container the player keeps. Acid goes one
    litre per container, so the litres a battery gives back when stripped are the ones it takes
    when rebuilt, in bottles of BatteryElectrolyte or anything else that holds a litre.

    Learning: NeedToBeLearn recipes come from the Homestead Power magazines, from AutoLearnAny,
    or from researching a found item (the item's output recipe, plus its ResearchableRecipes).
    Unmounting a panel, taking gadgets apart and servicing a battery need no learning.
*/
